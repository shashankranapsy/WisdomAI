About WisdomAI

WisdomAI is an educational scripture exploration project. It uses multilingual semantic search to find Bhagavad Gita verses that may relate to a situation described by a user.

The original verse, transliteration, translations and available explanations are displayed separately from the application's reflection on a modern situation.

Interpretations may differ across traditions and commentaries. WisdomAI is not a definitive religious authority.
